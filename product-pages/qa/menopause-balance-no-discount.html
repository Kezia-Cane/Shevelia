How do I take it?

Take two capsules together once daily with a meal, following the directions on your bottle. Each bottle contains 60 vegan capsules: a 30-day supply.

When should I assess my routine?

The product directions recommend consistent use over 8–12 weeks. Individual experiences vary. A supply length is not a promise of results; speak with a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms persist or you have concerns.

Does hormone-free mean suitable for everyone?

No. Hormone-free describes the formula, not its suitability for every person. This product is not HRT or a substitute for prescribed care. Review the full label with your clinician, especially if you take medication or have a hormone-sensitive condition.

Who should check before using it?

Not for anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing. Do not use if you have or have had a hormone-sensitive condition or liver disorder. Ask a healthcare professional about medication, HRT and other supplements before use. Read all warnings on your bottle.
