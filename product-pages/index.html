How do I take it?

Take two capsules together once daily with a meal, following the directions on your bottle. Each bottle contains 60 vegetarian capsules: a 30-day supply.

When should I assess my routine?

The product directions recommend consistent use over 8–12 weeks. Individual experiences vary. A supply length is not a promise of results; speak with a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms persist or you have concerns.

Is Daily Slim a weight-loss treatment?

No. Daily Slim is a dietary supplement for digestive, appetite and metabolic support. It does not replace balanced meals, movement or medical care, and it does not guarantee weight loss.

Who should check before using it?

Contains milk and soy. Contains green tea extract; take with food. Not for anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing. Ask a healthcare professional before use, especially with blood sugar, blood pressure or blood-thinning medication. Read all warnings on your bottle.

No. Every offer on this page is a one-time purchase. You receive the supply you select, with no automatic refill or recurring charge.
