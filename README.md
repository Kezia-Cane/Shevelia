# Shevelia landing page concepts

Three responsive landing page directions for client review:

1. **Calm product** — a warm, spacious Nightform story.
2. **The formula edit** — a Menopause Balance product and ingredient story.
3. **The ritual** — a visual introduction to the Shevelia brand.

Open `index.html` to compare the concepts. The pages use Shevelia brand colors, supplied product photography, and original lifestyle images created for this project.

These are design previews. Product details and purchase controls are not connected to Shopify. Check final product names, labels, claims, prices, and purchase terms against the live store before launch.
