Shevelia support

Returns, made clear.

Refund Policy · Last updated September 15, 2026

We do not offer a money-back or satisfaction guarantee. We do offer a clear return window for unopened product and a straightforward fix if something arrives wrong.

01 / Refunds

A simple path if something is not right.

1. Return eligibility

Unopened bottles can be returned within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date for a full refund of the product price.

  • Request within 30 days of the delivery date shown in carrier tracking.
  • Bottle is unused, seal intact, and in its original box.
  • Contact us first and receive return instructions.

2. What cannot be returned

  • Opened bottles or bottles with a broken seal.
  • Requests made more than 30 days after delivery.
  • Product purchased anywhere other than shevelia.co.

3. How to start a return

Email info@shevelia.co with your order number and how many bottles you are returning. We reply within 3 business days with the return address and instructions. Do not send anything back before you hear from us.

4. Return shipping

Return shipping is paid by the customer. Use a tracked service and keep the receipt.

5. Refund processing

Once your return arrives and we have checked the seal, we issue the refund within 5 business days to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may take additional days to post it.

6. Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

Email info@shevelia.co within 30 days with your order number and, for damage, a photo. We replace the item or refund it at our choice, and you do not need to return anything.

7. Cancellations

You can cancel an order for a full refund as long as it has not shipped. Once shipped, standard return terms apply.

8. Delivery confirmation

Carrier tracking that shows your parcel as delivered to the address you provided establishes the delivery date for this policy.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The version posted at the time of your order applies to that order.
