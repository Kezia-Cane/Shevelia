Privacy Policy · Last updated September 15, 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

01 / Privacy

Clear context for your information.

1. Information we collect

NSH Products LLC (Shevelia), 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, collects information you give us, including your name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address, order details, customer-service messages, and reviews or photos you choose to submit. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

We also collect IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring site, time spent, and interactions with emails and ads through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies. We do not ask for health information to sell you a product. If you voluntarily share it, we use it only to respond or, with consent, publish your review.

2. How we use it

We process and ship orders, send confirmations, provide customer service, prevent fraud, improve the Site and products, send marketing if you opt in, comply with legal obligations, and measure advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Service providers working on our behalf include Shopify and payment processors, our US fulfillment warehouse and carriers, email and customer-service tools, and analytics providers. Advertising partners such as Meta help measure ads and show ads to visitors. We may disclose information when required by law or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Cookies and tracking

Cookies and pixels support the cart, site functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can block cookies in your browser, though some Site features may not work. We honor opt-out preference signals where state law requires it.

5. Retention and security

We keep information as needed to fulfill orders, provide support, meet tax and legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Unsubscribe through any marketing email. To opt out of targeted advertising, email info@shevelia.co with the subject “Opt out” or adjust cookie preferences. Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information. We may verify your identity first and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

7. Children and international visitors

The Site and products are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The Site is operated from the United States and intended for US customers; information may be processed in the US.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on the Site. Questions or requests can be sent to info@shevelia.co
