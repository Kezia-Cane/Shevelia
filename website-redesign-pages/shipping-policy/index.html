Straightforward delivery details so you know what to expect from checkout to your door.

01 / Shipping

Delivery details for every order.

Where we ship

We currently ship within the United States only.

Shipping cost

Free standard shipping on every order. No minimum.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays.

Delivery time

Standard delivery takes 3 to 10 business days after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed.

Tracking

You receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. If you have not received it, contact us at info@shevelia.co.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check your shipping address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable, we contact you to arrange reshipment. Reshipment to a corrected address is free of charge once.

Damaged or missing parcels

If your order arrives damaged, or tracking shows delivered and you have not received it, email us at info@shevelia.co within 30 days of the delivery date and we will resolve it.
