## Opening

Today I will walk through the finalized Shevelia website redesign, the changes made from the live feedback, and the production checks completed before this presentation.

## Main Website Changes

### Global Design System

- Applied the Shevelia logo, favicon, colors, typography, spacing, and visual language consistently.
- Standardized the promotional banner, navigation, footer, utility icons, and payment badges.
- Simplified the main navigation to Home, About, Products, and Contact.
- Updated purchase-focused buttons to the Shevelia yellow brand color.
- Removed placeholder and internal review content.
- Removed em dashes and en dashes from the website copy.
- Increased text readability for middle-aged and older customers.

### Homepage

- Reworked the messaging into a more direct customer narrative.
- Added clearer paths to Daily Slim and Menopause Balance.
- Updated product descriptions, benefits, imagery, and calls to action.
- Improved product card alignment and left-aligned supporting content for easier reading.
- Added a clearer ingredient experience using the official product label images.
- Added product-focused FAQs based on the two existing Shopify product pages.
- Updated lifestyle images to feel more natural, relevant, and realistic.
- Improved the final call-to-action section with stronger image composition and spacing.

### About Page

- Simplified the hero content and removed unnecessary supporting labels.
- Replaced selected images with the approved brand assets.
- Improved the product story and made the layout more responsive.
- Removed the scrolling principles strip and other elements that distracted from the brand story.
- Adjusted image sizing so the product photography is displayed clearly without unwanted cropping.

### Contact Page

- Matched the typography and visual system used on the homepage.
- Replaced the hero image with the approved two-woman lifestyle image.
- Adjusted the image position so both women remain visible.
- Simplified the contact message and quick-answer section.
- Improved the FAQ typography and spacing for consistency and readability.

### Products Page

- Created a new product choice page for Daily Slim and Menopause Balance.
- Added the Products link to the global navigation.
- Used the approved product photography for both formulas.
- Rewrote the content to speak directly to the customer and how she wants to feel.
- Added relevant benefit icons for each product.
- Added clear yellow purchase buttons linking to the existing Shopify product pages.
- Fixed product image dimensions so the cards remain balanced without overlap or distortion.
- Added polished entrance animations and reduced-motion accessibility support.

### Policy and Support Pages

- Added redesigned Shipping, Refund, Privacy, Terms of Service, and Intellectual Property Rights pages.
- Retained the existing Shevelia policy content while applying the new design system.
- Linked the pages from the global footer.
- Matched the header icons and footer payment badges to the homepage.
- Corrected contact button contrast and shared page styling.

### Promotional Experience

- Updated the Black Friday banner with shorter, production-ready promotional copy.
- Improved the banner layout for desktop and mobile screens.
- Added a branded Black Friday promotional popup.
- Included clear paths to both Daily Slim and Menopause Balance.

### Animation and Interaction

- Added a branded introductory curtain animation with the Shevelia logo.
- Timed the hero content to begin after the introductory animation.
- Added polished content and product-card entrance animations.
- Added reduced-motion support for customers who prefer limited animation.
- Ensured only one testimonial video can play at a time.

## Responsive and Production Checks

## Suggested Presentation Flow

1. Begin with the homepage and explain the new customer-first narrative.
2. Show the two product paths and the clearer benefits for each formula.
3. Open the ingredient guide and show the official product label images.
4. Present the About page and explain the simplified brand story.
5. Present the Contact page and its improved quick-answer experience.
6. Present the new Products page and demonstrate the purchase paths.
7. Show the responsive mobile navigation and product card layout.
8. Briefly show the redesigned policy pages and consistent footer system.
9. Confirm that the site is ready for Shopify integration and final client approval.
